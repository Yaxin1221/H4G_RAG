   - Youth/young adults refusing gang recruitment (sometimes gendered: “young female students”, abandoned children);
   - Witnesses or informants against gangs;

   - Corporate owners refusing extortion demands.

- In **the U.S.** after repeal of the Trump-era policy (June 2021), courts are again reviewing gang‑based claims on their merits rather than treating them as a categorical bar.

1.  **Corroborating Allegations:** It establishes the degree of consistency between physical or psychological injuries and the alleged torture or ill-treatment. 3.  **Evidencing Impact of Return:** It details ongoing treatment needs and the potential impact of removal on the claimant’s physical or mental well-being, particularly if necessary treatment is unavailable in the country of origin.
